The premise

The tooth is built by epithelium, neural crest, and mesoderm, then asked to chew while surrounded by microbes. Immunity protects it — and decides how it repairs.

Kishen Lab is led by a clinician-scientist trained in both the clinic and biomedical engineering. For two decades the group has engineered biologically based approaches to bacterial biofilm-mediated infection, rather than treating the tooth as a cavity to be filled.
